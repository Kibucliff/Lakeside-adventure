About us

Built by the community, for unforgettable experiences.

Lakeside Adventures is a community-based eco-adventure organization in Kisumu, Kenya. We connect locals and visitors to the natural beauty, culture, and stories of Lake Victoria through welcoming, affordable, and memorable guided experiences.

Our mission is to make adventure accessible, responsible, and rooted in community wellbeing, conservation, and shared joy.

Eco-first Responsible access to nature and conservation-minded plans.
Community driven Locally led experiences with genuine cultural connection.
Wellness focused Adventures that support mental wellbeing and group connection.
Accessible pricing Fair packages designed for families, friends, and travelers.
